LUCK = wins − all-play expected wins (the share of the league you outscored each week); the same number the cumulative chart ends on.
±SD = that luck in standard deviations from zero. Beyond ±2 is rare (about 1 in 20).
Z LUCK = wins − expected wins from each week's score z-score, so the margin counts, not just the rank. Z LUCK above LUCK means you tend to win the close weeks.
SCORE Z = your average weekly score against the league that week, in SDs (closer to skill than luck).
OPP Z = the same for your opponents. A plus means you drew opponents on their hot weeks.
Ties count as half a win. A manager with no completed season shows this season's graded weeks so far (marked, e.g. "2026 only"); everyone else's row is completed regular seasons only.
